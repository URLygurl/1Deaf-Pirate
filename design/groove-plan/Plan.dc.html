Sixteen weeks, four moves

The plan

Each phase has one deliverable, one finish line, and a list of things you are deliberately not doing. The not-doing column is the load-bearing one.

01 Week 1

Close one loop

Deliverable

NEIL, complete. SOUL.md, SKILLS.md, tools.yaml, agent.config.yaml — straight from the template, nothing invented.

Done when

Cory says hello on the NakedApp dash and NEIL answers — not you.

Not doing

The other fourteen agents. The HIVE. Any new skill.

02 Weeks 2–4

Two stages, not six

Deliverable

HIVE stages 1 and 2 only — Capture and Retrieval — wired to NEIL. Memory provider chosen by Friday of week 1; the delay costs more than a wrong pick.

Done when

NEIL holds a correction from last week without being told twice.

Not doing

Source-truth, permissions, feedback, execution. Week 6 problems at the earliest.

03 Weeks 5–8

Graduate one

Deliverable

Whatever Cory asks for most often, run open eight times, then locked closed: fixed IO, scoped tools, cheap model, on the backlog.

Done when

It runs on cron for a full week and you never open it.

Not doing

Graduating anything that has not been run eight times. Your own doc says eight.

04 Weeks 9–16

Repeat the bar

Deliverable

One agent per fortnight, same template, no new machinery. The skills library becomes a parts bin — a skill gets pulled only when an agent needs it.

Done when

Week 16 closes with seven agents live and the reveal order intact.

Not doing

Rebuilding the platform because you learned things on the way. Log it, ship the bar.
