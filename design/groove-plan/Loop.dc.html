The rhythm that keeps it

A groove is a bar you repeat

Not a solo. Three beats a week and one running underneath, whatever else is on fire.

Every day DIME asks Cory the two-track question — rage day, or keep-it-rollin' day — and plays both snippets.
Mon — Commit

One line in the roadmap file

Write the single thing that will be true by Friday. That file is currently one byte — the cheapest fix in either repo.

Wed — Cut

Remove one thing, with a date

Delete it, archive it, or park it with the date you will look again. Surface area only ever comes down on purpose.

Fri — Rule

One correction becomes a rule

Take something you got wrong this week into hive/5-feedback-loops. Run the loop by hand until it runs itself.

Sat & Sun — Rest

Two dead steps, on purpose

The silent steps are what make the lit ones a rhythm. A bar with every step filled is just noise.

Miss a beat and the bar still plays. Miss the bar and there is no groove.
