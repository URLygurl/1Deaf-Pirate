# Alien Weaponry (Reference)

Alien Weaponry is a New Zealand thrash/groove-metal band from Waipu, Northland, internationally recognised for performing much of their material in te reo Maori and for drawing on Maori history and identity. Signed to the Austrian metal label Napalm Records, they became one of the highest-profile NZ metal exports of their generation, helping bring NZ metal to a global audience.
