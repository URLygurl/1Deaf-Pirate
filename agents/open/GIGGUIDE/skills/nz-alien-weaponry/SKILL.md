# Alien Weaponry

Alien Weaponry is a New Zealand metal band that broke through internationally by fusing thrash and groove metal with lyrics in te reo Māori, drawing on Māori history and the band members' whakapapa. Formed by brothers Lewis and Henry de Jong, they became one of NZ's most visible metal exports, touring the world and putting te reo metal on the global map.
