# Rip It Up — Magazine History

Rip It Up was New Zealand's most influential music magazine for decades, launching in the late 1970s and becoming the national paper of record for local and international music, gigs, and record releases. It championed the emerging NZ scene — punk, post-punk, Flying Nun, and beyond — and trained a generation of music writers. This skill provides the cultural and historical context around the magazine.
