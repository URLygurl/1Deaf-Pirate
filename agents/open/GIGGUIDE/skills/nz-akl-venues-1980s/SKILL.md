# Auckland Live Venues of the 1980s

Auckland's 1980s live circuit ran on licensed pub-rock rooms, downtown cabarets, hotel band rooms, and a handful of all-ages and alternative spaces. This was the engine room for New Zealand post-punk, the Flying Nun roster's Auckland shows, and a thriving covers-and-originals pub scene. This skill helps GIGGUIDE answer questions about which rooms mattered, what they were known for, and how the geography of the scene worked.

## Instructions
1. Identify whether the user wants a specific venue, a neighbourhood, a band's haunts, or a general overview of the 1980s circuit.
2. Anchor answers in the well-documented pillars of the era: the Gluepot (Ponsonby), Mainstreet Cabaret (Queen Street area), and the downtown/Karangahape Road cluster of pubs and clubs.
3. Distinguish venue types: pub-rock band rooms (hotel public bars), cabarets/nightclubs, and all-ages or alternative spaces.
4. When you name a venue, give its rough location and what it was known for (genre, scene, notable residencies) rather than inventing exact dates or addresses you are not certain of.
5. Cite AudioCulture's venue-mapping article as the canonical reference and recommend it for street-level detail.
6. If the user asks for a precise date, lineup, or address you cannot confirm, say so and keep the claim general.

## Examples
- User: "What was the Gluepot?" → Explain it was a Ponsonby hotel (Ponsonby/Three Lamps area) whose public bar became one of Auckland's defining live-rock rooms in the late 1970s and 1980s, central to pub rock and the originals scene.
- User: "Where did bands play in downtown Auckland in the 80s?" → Describe the Queen Street / lower-town cluster including Mainstreet Cabaret, plus the Karangahape Road end, and note the mix of cabarets and pub rooms.
- User: "Map the 1980s Auckland scene for me." → Give a short geography: Ponsonby (Gluepot), central city/Queen St (Mainstreet and cabarets), K' Road, and the hotel circuit, then point to the AudioCulture article.

## Guidelines
- Do anchor on the Gluepot and Mainstreet as the most reliably documented landmarks.
- Don't fabricate opening/closing dates, capacities, or exact addresses; keep uncertain specifics general.
- Do connect venues to scenes (Flying Nun shows, pub rock, post-punk) where it helps the user.
- Note that the licensing and drinking culture of the era shaped where and how bands played.
- Output: a tight, scene-aware answer with venue type and rough location, ending with the canonical reference.
