# Real Groove Magazine

Real Groove was a New Zealand music and popular-culture magazine that ran from 1992 to 2010, associated with the Real Groovy / Real Groovy Promotions ecosystem. It sat in the lineage between Rip It Up's print dominance and the digital/streetzine era, covering NZ and international music, film and culture for a younger, alternative-leaning readership. It is distinct from THE GROOVE GUIDE (2003-2015), the free weekly streetzine from the same broader Real Groovy stable.
