# Real Groovy Records

Real Groovy is one of New Zealand's best-known independent record stores, a long-running Auckland institution centred on Queen Street that became a destination for new and second-hand records, CDs, vinyl, and music ephemera. It outlasted most of its peers and remains a landmark in NZ music retail culture. This skill helps GIGGUIDE discuss the store's history and significance.

## Instructions
1. Establish Real Groovy as an Auckland independent that grew from second-hand trading into a large, well-known store associated with the Queen Street area.
2. Emphasise its dual stock model: both new releases and a deep second-hand/used catalogue across formats (vinyl, CD, and beyond).
3. Note its cultural role as a destination shop, employer of music-scene figures, and a survivor while many inner-city record stores closed.
4. If asked for an exact founding year, address, or ownership detail you cannot confirm, keep the claim general and point to the canonical source.
5. Cite the AudioCulture Real Groovy article as the reference; relate it to nz-lost-record-stores when discussing the wider retail picture.
