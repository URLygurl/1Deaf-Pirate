# UnderTheRadar — NZ Gig Guide

UnderTheRadar (undertheradar.co.nz) is New Zealand's main independent music site and gig guide, listing upcoming live shows nationwide, selling tickets, and covering local music news and reviews. It is GIGGUIDE's go-to source for *current* gig information, in contrast to the historical AudioCulture-based skills. This skill helps surface and frame live-listing queries.

## Instructions
1. Treat UnderTheRadar as the current/forward-looking gig source: upcoming shows, tours, and ticketing across NZ cities.
2. When a user asks what's on, clarify the city and date range (e.g. Auckland this weekend, Wellington next month) before pointing to or summarising listings.
3. Because listings are time-sensitive and you may not have live data, direct users to the gig guide URL for authoritative, up-to-date dates and ticket links rather than asserting specific shows from memory.
4. Distinguish this clearly from historical questions — if the user actually wants scene/venue history, route to nz-akl-venues-1980s, nz-audioculture, etc.
5. Cite the UnderTheRadar gig guide page as canonical.

## Examples
- User: "What gigs are on in Auckland this weekend?" → Clarify the date window and direct them to the UnderTheRadar gig guide for live, accurate listings; don't invent specific shows.
- User: "Where can I buy tickets to NZ shows?" → Point to UnderTheRadar as a primary independent ticketing/listing site.
- User: "Is there a NZ gig guide?" → Yes — UnderTheRadar; describe its national coverage and ticketing role.

## Guidelines
- Do flag that real-time listings live on the site and dates change; avoid stating specific upcoming shows as fact without live data.
- Don't confuse current listings with historical content; route history questions elsewhere.
- Do ask for city and timeframe to give a focused pointer.
- Output: a clarifying question if needed, plus the canonical gig-guide link.
