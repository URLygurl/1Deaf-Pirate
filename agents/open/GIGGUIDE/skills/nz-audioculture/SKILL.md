# AudioCulture — The Noisy Library of New Zealand Music

AudioCulture is the leading online encyclopaedia of New Zealand popular music history, hosting profiles of artists, labels, venues, scenes, and industry figures, plus long-form articles and photo archives. It is the canonical research hub behind most of GIGGUIDE's NZ-history skills. This skill explains what AudioCulture is and helps route users to the right material.

## Instructions
1. Describe AudioCulture as a curated, editorially-driven online encyclopaedia of NZ music history — artist profiles, scene and venue histories, label stories, and photo galleries.
2. Use it as a routing hub: when a user's question maps to a dedicated skill (nz-real-groovy, nz-rip-it-up-history, nz-alien-weaponry, nz-akl-venues-1980s, nz-lost-record-stores), invoke or recommend that skill.
3. When the topic is a NZ artist, label, or scene not covered by a dedicated skill, recommend searching AudioCulture and frame what kind of entry to expect (profile vs. article).
4. Be clear that AudioCulture is a secondary/curated source — good for narrative and context — and pair it with primary sources or Wikipedia for hard dates when needed.
5. Cite the AudioCulture homepage as the canonical entry point.

## Examples
- User: "What is AudioCulture?" → Explain it's the online encyclopaedia of NZ music history, with artist profiles, scene histories, and archives.
- User: "Where should I research a 1980s Dunedin band?" → Point them to AudioCulture profiles/articles and, if Auckland venues come up, to nz-akl-venues-1980s.
- User: "Find me the AudioCulture page on Real Groovy." → Hand off to nz-real-groovy and note the canonical article URL.

## Guidelines
- Do treat this as the hub that connects the other NZ-history skills.
- Don't duplicate the detailed content of the dedicated skills; route to them.
- Do note AudioCulture is curated/editorial, strong on narrative and photos.
- Output: an orientation plus a routing recommendation, ending with the canonical reference.

Canonical reference: https://www.audioculture.co.nz/
