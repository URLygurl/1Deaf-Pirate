# Proven Release Strategies & Tactics

This is the working library of release tactics that consistently move the needle for independent artists. Where rollout-strategy lays out the calendar, this skill supplies the proven moves that fill it — pre-save campaigns, single-led discovery, editorial and algorithmic playlist pitching, content teasers, and fan-base activation — and helps the user choose the right ones for their stage and goals.

## Instructions
1. Clarify the goal before recommending tactics: streams, saves/follows, playlist placement, fan growth, or revenue — each weights the tactic mix differently.
2. Recommend a pre-save / pre-add campaign for every release: set the link live at announcement, drive it everywhere, because pre-saves signal demand to streaming editorial and convert to day-one streams.
3. Lead with singles: release one or more singles ahead of an EP/album so each is a discovery on-ramp and a chance to earn editorial and algorithmic traction before the full body lands.
4. Pitch playlists deliberately: submit to Spotify for Artists editorial at least 7 days before release (4 weeks ideal), then layer algorithmic (Release Radar, Discover Weekly via saves) and independent curator outreach.
5. Build a teaser content cycle: short-form clips (hooks, BTS, lyric snippets, visualizers) staged before and through release to seed the song and train the algorithm on engagement.
6. Activate owned audience: email list and DM/close-friends pushes convert hardest — schedule a fan email at announcement, on release day, and in the week after.
7. Use momentum mechanics: sustain engagement across release week (not one spike), encourage saves and adds over passive plays, and re-trigger interest with follow-up content.
8. Set measurable targets (pre-save count, day-one saves, playlist adds, follower delta) and define how each tactic will be tracked.
9. Return a prioritized tactic stack matched to the artist's stage, with the highest-leverage 3–5 moves called out first.

## Examples
- "How do I get more pre-saves before my single?" → Give the pre-save playbook: live link at announce, link-in-bio everywhere, fan email, teaser CTA each post, and a count target; explain why pre-saves drive day-one streams and editorial signals.
- "What actually works to promote an indie release?" → Deliver a prioritized stack: pre-save, single-led discovery, editorial + algorithmic playlist pitch, teaser content, fan-list activation — top 3 highlighted for their stage.
- "How do I pitch Spotify editorial?" → Walk through Spotify for Artists submission, the 7-day-minimum / 4-week-ideal lead, picking one strong unreleased track, and pairing with saves to unlock Release Radar/Discover Weekly.
- "I have a small following — which tactics matter most?" → Concentrate on owned-audience activation, pre-saves, and one strong single with tight teaser content; de-prioritize broad paid pushes until there's a base to retarget.

## Guidelines
- Tie every tactic to the stated goal; don't hand a streams-focused artist a follower-growth stack.
- Pre-save first, always — it's the cheapest, highest-signal move and it gates several downstream mechanics.
- Respect the editorial lead time: a pitch submitted under 7 days out is effectively too late.
- Favor saves, adds, and follows over raw play counts; engagement signals are what algorithms reward.
- Don't recommend a tactic the artist can't execute or measure; right-size to their resources and stage.
- Owned audience converts better than borrowed reach — never skip the email list and DM push.
