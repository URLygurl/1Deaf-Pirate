# Rollout Timeline (Teaser → Release Schedule)

This skill turns a release plan into a precise, dated timeline: every teaser, pre-save push, content drop, email, and platform action placed on a specific day relative to release. Where rollout-strategy decides the phases, this skill produces the calendar artifact the artist can actually follow — typically a 6–8 week EP timeline or a 3–4 week single timeline counted down to release day.

## Instructions
1. Capture the release date and format; everything is dated relative to "Release Day" (R-day).
2. Pick the window: 6–8 weeks for an EP/album, 3–4 weeks for a single. Count backward in weeks (Week -8 … Week -1, Release Week, Week +1 … +4).
3. Lay down the fixed milestones first: distribution upload (R minus 3–4 weeks), pre-save link live (announcement), editorial playlist pitch (R minus 7 days minimum / 4 weeks ideal), and release day itself.
4. Place the teaser cycle: an early "something's coming" tease, a project announcement with artwork + date, then escalating snippet/BTS/visualizer beats roughly 2–3 per week into release.
5. Schedule pre-save activation: link live at announce, then recurring CTAs (every content post, plus a dedicated fan email at announce and mid-campaign).
6. Detail Release Week day-by-day: R-day (post, story takeover, email, pre-save conversion), R+1 and R+2 reinforcement, R+4 follow-up clip — sustaining engagement across the week.
7. Add the post-release tail: Weeks +1 to +4 with alternate edits, live/performance clips, playlist and sync follow-up, and a recap.
8. Assign each row an owner, an asset, and a channel; mark dependencies (e.g. pre-save link blocks all pre-save CTAs).
9. Output as a clean dated table or checklist, ready to drop into a calendar or task board.

## Examples
- "Build me a week-by-week timeline for my EP out Sept 12." → Produce a backward-counted 8-week table from Week -8 to Week +4 with milestones, teaser beats, pre-save pushes, release-week day-by-day, and the tail.
- "When should I post my first teaser and pitch playlists?" → Place the first tease around Week -4 to -3, the announcement before it for pre-saves, and the editorial pitch at R minus 7 days minimum (4 weeks ideal); show them on the timeline.
- "Give me a tight 3-week single rollout calendar." → Compress to Weeks -3/-2/-1 plus release week, front-loading pre-save and teaser beats and a focused R-day push.
- "What happens the week of release, day by day?" → Lay out R-day, R+1, R+2, R+4 actions with channels and assets to keep engagement sustained rather than spiked.

## Guidelines
- Always output actual dates or clear R-relative offsets — a timeline without days is not a timeline.
- Front-load the binding lead times: distribution upload and the 7-day editorial pitch window are hard constraints; schedule around them first.
- Keep teaser frequency realistic (2–3 beats/week) and ensure the content exists or is batched before the schedule starts.
- Sustain release week across multiple days; don't collapse everything onto R-day.
- Make dependencies explicit so a missing pre-save link or late upload doesn't silently break the plan.
- Hand strategic "why" and phase logic to rollout-strategy, tactic depth to release-strategies, and format choice to waterfall-strategy.
