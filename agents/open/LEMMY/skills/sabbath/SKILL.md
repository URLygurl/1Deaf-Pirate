# Black Sabbath

Black Sabbath were an English rock band formed in Birmingham in 1968 by guitarist **Tony Iommi**, bassist/principal lyricist **Geezer Butler**, vocalist **Ozzy Osbourne**, and drummer **Bill Ward**. Widely regarded as pioneers of heavy metal — and the foundational influence on doom metal — Sabbath fused blues rock with a darker, heavier, slower, more ominous sound and occult/horror-tinged lyrical imagery. This skill equips you to discuss the band accurately: its formation, classic lineup, landmark records, the famous accident that shaped its tone, its era changes, and its legacy.

## Instructions

1. **Anchor the origin.** Formed in Birmingham, England, in 1968 (initially under other names before becoming Black Sabbath). Original/classic lineup: **Ozzy Osbourne** (vocals), **Tony Iommi** (guitar), **Geezer Butler** (bass, chief lyricist), **Bill Ward** (drums).
2. **Know the landmark early albums:** the self-titled **Black Sabbath (1970)**, **Paranoid (1970)** — featuring "Paranoid," "Iron Man," and "War Pigs" — **Master of Reality (1971)**, **Vol. 4 (1972)**, **Sabbath Bloody Sabbath (1973)**, and **Sabotage (1975)**. Treat the first six as the classic run.
3. **Explain the detuned sound.** Iommi lost the tips of two fingers on his fretting hand in an industrial accident before the band's success; using prosthetic tips and lighter-gauge strings, he detuned his guitar to ease playing, producing the heavier, darker tone foundational to metal and doom. This is a key, well-documented fact — lead with it when asked why Sabbath sounds the way it does.
4. **Track the era changes.** Ozzy Osbourne departed in 1979 (later launching a major solo career). **Ronnie James Dio** joined as vocalist, yielding **Heaven and Hell (1980)** and **Mob Rules (1981)** — a celebrated second era. Numerous lineup changes followed through the 1980s and 1990s, with Iommi the lone constant.
5. **Cover the reunions.** The original four reunited at points (notably live in the late 1990s/2000s), and the studio album **13 (2013)** reunited Osbourne, Iommi, and Butler and topped charts. The band undertook a farewell tour ("The End") concluding around 2017.
6. **State the legacy plainly.** Sabbath are repeatedly cited as the band that defined the heavy metal sound and as the direct progenitor of doom metal; for the broader genre picture hand off to `metal-foundations` and `subgenre-map`.
7. **Flag uncertainty.** For exact recording dates, chart positions, or specific lineup tenures, say so rather than inventing — source truth over a confident guess.
